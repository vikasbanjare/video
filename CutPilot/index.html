✨ Clean up my videoone tap

      
Removes all the dead air — silence at the start, middle and end —
         and every repeated take, even when you re-read a script many times without stopping
         the camera. The AI reads your transcript, keeps your best (final) take of each line and
         cuts the earlier attempts + any off-script talk — all in one pass. Captions stay in sync, so you
         never re-transcribe.

Did a few retakes of the same line? Pulse reads your
           transcript, finds where you restarted a phrase, keeps the best
           take and ripple-deletes the earlier ones. Transcribe your clip first
           (Transcribe tab) — word timing makes the cuts tight.

        
How hard to look for retakes?

        

          
          
          
        

        
Strong also catches reworded retakes;
           Gentle only obvious repeats. Always keeps your last (best) take.

Verbatim transcriptionfor retake removal

      
Removing retakes needs a transcript that keeps every re-read — the normal
         engine (Groq) cleans those away. Add a Deepgram or AssemblyAI key (both have free
         tiers) and Pulse uses it just for the “remove retakes” pass.

      
      
      

    

    

      
Audio enhancementcleaner = more accurate

      
Before transcribing, Pulse runs a quick voice clean-up (drops low rumble & hiss) so the recogniser hears clearer speech — fewer wrong words, especially on noisy recordings. It only touches the copy sent to the recogniser; your media is never changed.

      
    

    

      
Word-by-word timingtighter highlight

      
Sharpen each word's start/end against the audio so the highlight rides the actual voice — snaps any word that drifted into a pause back onto the speech, and evens out long vs. short words. It's pure timing data, so your captions stay fully editable. (Self-checks the audio first; if it can't line up, it stays gentle.)
