✨ Clean up my video
        

      
Cuts silences, repeated takes and fillers. A backup comes first.

      
Removes dead air at the start, middle and end, and repeated takes — even a script you re-read many times without stopping. It keeps your last full take of each line and cuts earlier tries and off-script talk, in one pass. Pulse listens to every mic and only cuts where everyone is quiet. Captions stay in sync. For retakes it needs your words first (Transcript).

      How much to cut?
      

        
        
        
      

      
YouTube suits most talking videos. 

      
YouTube: pauses over 0.5s shortened to 0.3s. Reel: tight, punchy cuts. Podcast: keeps a natural rhythm (only pauses over 0.8s). Pulse measures each mic's own room noise, so there's no level to set. To target one clip, select it first.

🔁 Remove repeated takes
          

        
Keeps your last full take of each line.

        
Pulse reads your words — English, Hindi, Hinglish or a mix — finds where you restarted a line, keeps the last complete take and removes the earlier ones. On a podcast, an answer that repeats the other person's words is not a retake. Get your words first (Transcript) — word timing makes the cuts tight.

        How hard to look?
        

          
          
          
        

        
Strong also catches reworded retakes. You tick which cuts to make.

        
        
Podcast? Pick “Two or more people”. 

        
A retake is the same person saying a line again. Auto can only tell who is talking when the words have speaker labels; otherwise it plays safe. Pick Two or more people for a podcast so an answer that echoes the question is never cut, or Just me for a reel so every restart is caught.

        

          Expert settings
          

            
            
          

          
        

      

      
      
🎯 Best for a script read many times. Tip: mute your music track first. 

      
      
It listens again to every voice on your timeline (each mic of a podcast) with a word-for-word listener that keeps every take, tells speakers apart, keeps the last complete take of each line, snaps cuts to the pauses and lists them for you to check. Muted tracks, offline files and steady music are left out — it tells you which. Needs a retake-finder key (Settings).

      
✨ Also cuts false starts and “let me think” pauses. 

      
Smart Cleanup reads your whole transcript and also removes false starts, fillers (“um”, “aa”, a stand-alone “matlab”) and “let me think” dead air that silence-cutting can't see, then keeps the last complete take. You check every cut before anything is removed.

🎬 Viral Shorts
        

      
Finds the best short clips in a long video.

      
Pulse reads your transcript and finds the moments most likely to pop as short clips — each one a self-contained hook → payoff. Get your words first (Transcript). For each moment you can preview it, mark it, or cut it out as a vertical clip — Premiere's Auto Reframe keeps the subject in frame.

      

        
        
        
      

      

        
        

      

    

    


    

      
🎙 Vertical that follows the speaker
        

      
For podcasts with a mic per person.

      
For 2–3 people, each on their own mic: Pulse follows whoever's talking — one person when one holds the floor, split-screen when they talk over each other. It uses your separate audio tracks and the shape chosen above, and puts a finished vertical clip back in your project.
