========================================================
  CutPilot — Install & Setup Guide (for Premiere Pro, Mac)
  Version 0.9.98
========================================================

CutPilot is a panel that lives inside Adobe Premiere Pro. It adds
auto-captions (it can transcribe your video by itself), Smart Cut,
Multicam, and Chapters.

This guide is written for first-time users. Follow it top to bottom.


--------------------------------------------------------
  WHAT YOU NEED
--------------------------------------------------------
- A Mac (macOS).
- Adobe Premiere Pro 2020 or newer.
- An internet connection (only for the one-time engine setup).
- The file:  CutPilot-v0.9.98.zip


--------------------------------------------------------
  PART 1 — INSTALL THE CUTPILOT PANEL
--------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1.  QUIT Premiere Pro completely.
         (Top menu: Premiere Pro > Quit, or press  Cmd + Q.)
         Closing the window is NOT enough — it must fully quit.

STEP 2.  Double-click  CutPilot-v0.9.98.zip  to unzip it.
         You now have a folder named  CutPilot.

STEP 3.  Open the CutPilot folder. Find the file:
             install-mac.command
         RIGHT-CLICK it  >  Open.
         (Right-click — not double-click. If a warning appears,
          click "Open" to confirm. This is normal for free tools.)

STEP 4.  A black Terminal window opens. It says
             "Installing CutPilot v0.9.98..."
         Press  Enter  when it asks.
         If macOS asks for permission to your Desktop/Documents/
         Downloads, click  OK / Allow.


--------------------------------------------------------
  PART 2 — SET UP THE AUTO-CAPTION ENGINE (one time)
--------------------------------------------------------
This lets CutPilot transcribe your video by itself.

STEP 5.  The installer asks:
             "Set it up now? [Y/n]"
         Type  Y  and press  Enter.

STEP 6.  If your Mac does not already have "Homebrew", the installer
         installs it for you. macOS will ask for YOUR MAC PASSWORD —
         type it and press Enter. (You won't see dots while typing —
         that's normal. Just type and press Enter.)

STEP 7.  Wait. It downloads about 250 MB (ffmpeg + whisper + the
         speech model). This can take a few minutes. Do not close
         the window.

STEP 8.  When it finishes it prints "Engine status":
             ffmpeg : ...
             whisper: ...
             model  : ...
         If all three show a path (not "NOT found"), you're ready.
         Press  Enter  to close.

         (If any says "NOT found", see TROUBLESHOOTING below.)


--------------------------------------------------------
  PART 3 — OPEN CUTPILOT IN PREMIERE
--------------------------------------------------------
STEP 9.  Open Premiere Pro.

STEP 10. Top menu:  Window  >  Extensions  >  CutPilot.
         The CutPilot panel appears.

STEP 11. Check the very top of the panel — it must say  v0.9.98.
         If it shows an older number: fully quit Premiere (Cmd + Q)
         and open it again.


--------------------------------------------------------
  PART 4 — ADD CAPTIONS (basic flow)
--------------------------------------------------------
STEP 12. Open your project and a sequence with your video on the
         timeline.

STEP 13. In CutPilot, go to the  Captions  tab.

STEP 14. GET THE WORDS:
         - Click your talking clip on the timeline (so it's selected).
         - Click  "Auto-transcribe this clip".
         - Wait ~30-60 seconds. It fills in the words and shows how
           much it covered (e.g. 0:00-3:45).

STEP 15. PICK A LOOK:
         - "Caption styles" tab = built-in animated styles
           (change font, colour, animation).
         - "My .mogrt files" tab = upload your own Premiere template
           with  "+ Add .mogrt file", then tap it to edit its
           Text colour / Font / Size, and Preview.

STEP 16. Click the big caption button to add the captions to your
         timeline. Done.


--------------------------------------------------------
  TROUBLESHOOTING
--------------------------------------------------------
* "install-mac.command can't be opened"  →  Right-click the file >
  Open (instead of double-click), then click Open in the dialog.

* Panel still shows an OLD version  →  Quit Premiere fully (Cmd + Q),
  reopen. The number at the top must read v0.9.98.

* "Engine status" shows whisper or ffmpeg = NOT found  →  Open the
  CutPilot panel > Settings tab > "Auto-transcribe" section >
  tap "Install the engine for me". Or install Homebrew once from
  https://brew.sh and run install-mac.command again.

* Auto-transcribe says "select a clip"  →  Click your video/audio
  clip on the timeline first, then try again.

* Captions only cover part of the video  →  Make sure you selected
  the right (longest) talking clip, then Auto-transcribe again. Check
  the coverage time it reports matches your clip length.

* Colour shows wrong on a Premiere template  →  In the template's
  editor, tick "Fix red/blue swap". For exact colours with any font,
  the "Caption styles" (Animated) section always works.


--------------------------------------------------------
  NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------
- This setup is for Mac. Windows auto-setup is not included yet.
- Everything runs on your own computer. Your video is not uploaded
  anywhere for transcription.
- To update later: get the new CutPilot-vX.X.X.zip, unzip, and run
  install-mac.command again (your engine setup stays).
